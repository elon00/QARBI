# Jurisdiction Matrix

| Area | Automated project control | Human/legal decision required |
|---|---|---|
| India privacy/data | data-minimisation boundary and evidence checks | applicability, notices, consent/other lawful processing basis and operational obligations |
| India blockchain | truth/evidence controls and architecture documentation | exact regulatory classification of the product |
| Supreme Court of India | exact-case citation requirement | legal interpretation and applicability |
| FATF | risk-classification trigger documentation | whether the product/operator falls within applicable VA/VASP obligations |
| EU/MiCA | territorial/activity trigger documentation | applicability and authorization/operational obligations |

This matrix is deliberately conservative: **automation can detect missing evidence, but cannot certify legal compliance**.
