# ⚖️ GLOBAL & INDIAN BLOCKCHAIN COMPLIANCE RULES

This rule definition establishes continuous compliance across all projects:

1. **Supreme Court of India Directives:**
   - Follow *IAMAI v. RBI (2020)* principles on lawful blockchain research & deployment.
   - Adhere to PMLA 2002 & FIU-IND 2023 AML/CFT rules (sanctions check, velocity limits).
   - Ensure CERT-In 2022 timestamped audit logging standards.
   - Include ASCI 2022 mandatory risk disclaimers on Virtual Digital Assets.

2. **International Blockchain Standards:**
   - FATF Recommendation 16 (Travel Rule) sanctions blacklist filtering.
   - EU MiCA (Regulation EU 2023/1114) transparent disclosure & anti-manipulation.
   - NIST FIPS 204 (ML-DSA-65) post-quantum cryptographic standards.

3. **Truth & Reality Protocol:**
   - 100% genuine evidence; no fake mocks, simulation claims, or fabricated metrics.
